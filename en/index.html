How we transform a process

How DABIA transforms a process.

A short methodology and a simple example.

01. Analyze

Understand the process.

We observe how the team works today, where time is lost and which tasks consume resources without creating value.

02. Automate

Decide what to automate.

We connect systems and rules. We only automate what makes sense to automate, leaving room for people to apply judgment.

03. Measure

Confirm the improvement.

We compare before and after: time recovered, cost reduced, capacity freed. Decisions are made on data, not intuition.

04. Scale

Carry the same pattern to other processes.

Once validated, we replicate the approach in the next process. No over-engineering, no tool sprawl.

Illustrative example

How the hours add up.

If a business processes 300 requests a month and each one requires 8 minutes of manual work:

300 requests × 8 minutes=2,400 minutes=40 hours of manual work per month

The goal of the diagnostic is to identify how much of that work could realistically be reduced through automation.

It is not person versus automation.

It is person + automation.

Each part of the process should be done by whoever can add the most value to it.

Some decisions must stay human.

When money, sensitive information, risk, a meaningful exception or low certainty is involved, automation can pause and request review.

Automation Diagnostic

Find out what is worth automating.

We review your processes, identify where you lose time or money and determine which automation could have the biggest impact.

  • Current process. How it works today and where work piles up.
  • Time spent. Minutes or hours dedicated to each step.
  • Frequency. How often the process runs.
  • Errors. Points where it breaks or needs rework.
  • Systems involved. Tools, forms and data that participate.
  • Outcome we want to improve. The metric that matters to move.

With that information we estimate the time recoverable, the current cost of the process and the automation priority.
