# DABIA Brand Kit

## Brand
**DABIA** — AI & Business Automation

**Tagline:** Automatiza lo que frena tu negocio.

**Strategic line:** No empezamos con AI. Empezamos con el problema.

## Colors
- Midnight: `#070910`
- Panel Navy: `#101722`
- Dark Navy: `#071426`
- DABIA Violet: `#7C5CFF`
- Electric Blue: `#63B1FF`
- Automation Teal: `#2BD9A8`
- White: `#F7F9FC`
- Slate: `#9AA7B8`

Brand gradient: `#7C5CFF → #63B1FF → #2BD9A8`

Blue is dominant; violet represents AI/innovation; teal represents automation/result.

## Personality
Intelligent, reliable, modern, practical, measurable and human.
